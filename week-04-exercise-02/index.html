Week 04 / Exercise 02

My favorite games.

Six games I keep coming back to. From a quiet chessboard to a close team match, I love games that give me a new challenge every time.

THE COLLECTION 06 GAMES

SELECT 01

Balatro

Poker-inspired roguelike

I like how one Joker can change an entire run. Building combinations and seeing a score climb makes every hand feel like a new puzzle.

SELECT 02

Bloons TD 6

Tower defense

I enjoy experimenting with different towers and upgrades. Finding a setup that handles a difficult round makes the planning feel worth it.

SELECT 03

Undertale

Role-playing adventure

The characters, music, and choices make this game stand out to me. I like that an encounter can be about understanding someone as much as winning a fight.

SELECT 04

Chess

Board game · Strategy

I like slowing down and thinking a few moves ahead. Even with the same pieces and rules, every game gives me a different problem to solve.

SELECT 05

Tetris

Falling-block puzzle

Fitting the pieces together is simple to understand and hard to stop playing. Clearing lines feels satisfying, especially when the board starts filling up.

SELECT 06

Valorant

Tactical team shooter

I enjoy the mix of precise aim, agent abilities, and teamwork. Coordinating a round and making a good play with the team keeps matches exciting.

Good games. Different challenges. One collection.
